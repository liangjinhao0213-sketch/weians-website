02How an order runs

From drawing to container,
in five steps.

The same five steps run on every order, whether it is one door for a villa or four hundred units for a project. Nothing moves to the next step until the previous one is signed off.

Step 01 — Site check

Enquiry

You send drawings, sizes or a photo of the opening. We confirm what the wall is made of, how the door has to drain and what the site can carry.

Opening schedule reviewed against the drawing

Step 02 — 24 hours

Quotation

A factory-direct price in 24 hours: profile, glass build-up, hardware, packing and the Incoterm you asked for, itemised line by line.

Itemised quotation with the Incoterm stated

Step 03 — Signed drawing

Design

Our engineers return shop drawings and a cost-optimised alternative. Nothing is released to the floor until you sign the drawing.

Elevations, section, glazing and hardware schedule

Step 04 — Stage photos

Production

Extrusion, machining, powder coating and glazing on one site. You get photo updates at each stage, not a tracking number.

Extrusion, coating and glazing updates plus a QC sheet

Step 05 — Documents

Delivery

Panels are crated upright, loaded and sealed. We send the loading photos and the container number the day it sails.

Packing list, loading photos and the bill of lading

04Manufacturing

Own extrusion. Own fabrication.
Own quality gate.

10,000 m² under one roof in Foshan: extrusion, CNC machining, powder coating, insulated glazing and water testing — so tolerances do not depend on a third party.

01

Extrusion

6063-T6 billets extruded to profile in-house, aged and anodised or powder coated.

02

CNC machining

Five-axis centres cut drainage, hardware and glazing rebates to ±0.2 mm.

03

Powder coating

Nine-stage pre-treatment and automated line, 60–80 µm AkzoNobel finish.

04

IGU assembly

Low-E, argon-filled double and triple glazing, warm-edge spacer, desiccant filled.

05

QC & water test

Air, water and wind testing to AS 2047 / EN 14351 before every shipment.

06

Packing

Foam-cradled, corner-protected, container-optimised crating for sea freight.
