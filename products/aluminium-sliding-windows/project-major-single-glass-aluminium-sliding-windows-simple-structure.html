What this system does.
How it is built.

Simple Structure Project Major Single Glass Aluminium Sliding Window

  • Aluminum Frame — The main structure of the window, providing support and durability. Aluminum is lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and strong, making it ideal for window frames.
  • Single Glass Pane — A single sheet of glass that allows light to pass through. Unlike double-glazed windows, these do not have a gap for insulation, making them less energy-efficient.
  • Sliding Mechanism — Consists of rollers or glides at the bottom of the moving sash, allowing it to slide smoothly along a bottom track. This mechanism is simple and reliable.
  • Track: A channel built into the bottom of the frame where the rollers move. The track guides the sliding sash.
  • Sash: The part of the window that holds the glass. In a single sliding window, there is one moving sash and one fixed or stationary sash.
  • Space-Saving — They don't require any outward or inward swinging space, making them ideal for areas with limited room, like patios or walkways.
