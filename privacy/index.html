Who we are

Foshan Weinas Energy Saving Curtain Wall Doors and Windows Technology Co., Ltd. (“Weinas”, “we”) manufactures aluminium doors and windows at No. 7, Dalingdi, Zhongxiu Second Village Group, Tangbian Village, Shishan Town, Foshan, Guangdong, China. This page explains what we do with the information you send us.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website we receive the name, company, email address, WhatsApp number, project type and message you type into the form, plus the page you sent it from. We do not ask for, and do not want, payment card details through this form.

Our server records the IP address that submitted the form for thirty seconds so that repeated submissions can be blocked. That record is used only for spam prevention.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry: to prepare a quotation, to check a specification against a drawing, to arrange samples, and to keep the commercial record of what was agreed. We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and on your consent for any follow-up marketing.

How long we keep it

Quotation requests are kept for twenty-four months from the last exchange, so that a re-order can be priced against the same specification. Anything older is deleted. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time and we will do so within thirty days.

Who sees it

Our sales and engineering staff in Foshan, and nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade contact details. We do not run third-party advertising or tracking scripts on this site.

Cookies

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. If your browser stores a session for the enquiry form it expires when you close the tab.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to liangjinhao0213@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request” and we will reply within thirty days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page. The version above is the one in force from 1 November 2026.
